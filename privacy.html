PRIVACY
LESOCO PRIVACY COMMITMENT

Privacy built in from the first frame.

We believe biomechanical and athletic data belongs exclusively to you. Here is how LeSoCo protects your privacy in plain, transparent language.

1. Local-First Processing & Storage

All kinematic computations, joint degree calculations, and session recordings are executed locally on your computer.

  • No Cloud Uploads: Your .lsc motion files and snapshots remain stored on your local workstation by default.
  • Offline Operation: LeSoCo operates fully offline without requiring an active internet connection to capture or review takes.

2. Sensor & IMU Telemetry

When pairing with Sony Mocopi or compatible IMU hardware:

  • Real-Time Orientation Only: Telemetry streams (accelerometer, gyroscope, and quaternion rotations) are used solely to solve the real-time 3D avatar pose.
  • No Biometric Surveillance: We do not collect biological identifiers, facial recognition data, or personal biometric profiles.

3. Zero Third-Party Data Sharing

We respect your coaching, training, and clinical workflows:

  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your motion records to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.
  • No Hidden Telemetry: There are no advertising trackers, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics embedded within LeSoCo.

4. Your Data Ownership

You hold complete ownership of every motion file, annotation comment, and benchmark sequence created within LeSoCo:

  • Unrestricted Export: Export your .lsc files freely to your computer at any time.
  • Permanent Deletion: You have the right and ability to delete any clip, take, or session permanently from your local disk without residual retention.

5. Inquiries & Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your motion capture data, please contact the LeSoCo development team directly via our repository or support channels.

§ LeSoCo Version 1.3 is an active pre-release engineering branch. Motion capture analysis, 3D viewport rendering, and high-frequency IMU telemetry ingestion are provided for evaluation purposes. Features, user interface designs, and API contracts are subject to change prior to production release.

* Hardware compatibility: Wireless recording requires supported wearable IMU hardware (including Sony Mocopi 6-point and experimental 12-IMU configurations). Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5.0+) or direct UDP socket forwarding over a local 5GHz Wi-Fi network is required. Sensor precision, drift correction, and kinematic fidelity may vary depending on calibration quality and physical magnetic interference.

** System Requirements: 4K timeline scrubbing, multi-rig comparative visualization, and real-time biomechanical angular computation require macOS 13+ / Windows 10 (64-bit) or later with Metal or DirectX 12 hardware acceleration. A dedicated GPU with at least 4GB VRAM is recommended for high-resolution dual humanoid playback.

Telemetry & Local Privacy: LeSoCo does not transmit biometric capture files, motion recordings, or skeletal kinematics telemetry to external cloud servers. All recording processing, joint calculations, and timeline exports remain strictly local on your machine unless explicitly configured otherwise by your institutional environment.
