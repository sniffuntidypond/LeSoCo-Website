FRAME-BY-FRAME • RAW IMU TELEMETRY

Every frame counts.

A high-precision kinematic timeline scrubber featuring instant keyframe bookmarking, annotation navigation, and direct Raw IMU sensor stream diagnostics.

Frame-Accurate Navigation.

Scrub fluidly across milliseconds or jump directly between critical landmarks:

  • Digital Frame Counter: Pinpoint the exact frame index (e.g. Frame: 0) with zero lag.
  • Annotation Stepper Buttons: Use the < and > buttons to leap forward and backward between tagged keyframes.
  • Tag Pose Trigger: Press [+ Tag Pose] to insert persistent posture milestones at any frame.

Raw IMU Telemetry Mode.

Dive beneath the solved 3D skeleton to verify raw physical sensor metrics:

  • Raw IMU Toggle: Switch from solved joint rotations to raw 9-axis gyroscope and accelerometer data.
  • Drift Diagnostics: Detect magnetic distortion and sensor calibration discrepancies before they affect analysis.
  • Live Help Line: Embedded system readiness monitor confirming steady tracking at all times.
